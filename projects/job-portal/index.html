Job Portal

Full-stack Job Portal & Recruitment Management System with company profile and end-to-end hiring lifecycle.

Source Code

About this Project

A full-stack recruitment platform that covers the entire hiring lifecycle — from publishing job posts and collecting applications, to multi-step evaluation pipelines, multi-level approval workflows, and candidate re-engagement via a Talent Pool. Includes a public-facing company profile & careers page for employer branding. Built with Clean Architecture and CQRS on the backend (ASP.NET Core 10 + MediatR), and a React 19 SPA on the frontend. Authentication is handled by Keycloak with mandatory TOTP 2FA. Documents are stored in MinIO/S3 with presigned URLs. All branding — company name, logo, colors, and contact info — is configurable via the UI without redeployment.
